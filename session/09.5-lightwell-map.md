## 1. Purpose and observable outcome

Build one coherent, single-floor industrial-office map centered on a walkable lightwell. Three attackers must be able to choose, fake and switch between two plant sites. Three defenders must be able to gather information and rotate without one central position controlling the entire map. Wall destruction and player reinforcement must materially change those decisions.

At completion, six people can launch matching Mac/Windows exports, identify A and B, play both sides, plant/defuse at either site, breach and reinforce arbitrary wall locations, complete a match/rematch, and explain where they heard nearby speech. The map stays navigable and readable through several connected breaches and resets cleanly. Provide measured frame times and a short record of human findings, not a claim of final competitive balance.

The first implementation increment is an undecorated playable graybox. Do not produce all the art before route timing, site pressure and universal wall coverage work.

## 2. Requirements, proposals and exclusions

### Confirmed requirements

- **3v3**, keyboard/mouse, Mac and Windows, private matches with friends.
- **Two alternative plant sites, A and B**. Planting at one is sufficient; this is not a paired objective that requires both sites.
- **Every wall can be destroyed and every wall can be reinforced**, including the exterior shell and all four courtyard walls. There are no permanently solid wall chokepoints or privileged breach-only panels.
- Close-quarters rifle combat, listening/proximity speech, radios/theft, ordinary/heavy breach tools and counterplay through reinforcement.
- Grounded office/industrial atmosphere at night, warm practicals and cool exterior lighting, with combat visibility taking priority.
- Host-owned outcomes, consistent wall collision/visual state, bounded effects, reliable resets and a $0 asset budget.

### Proposed starting specification

The 32 × 28 m building, courtyard dimensions, 40 × 42 m playable ground envelope, all coordinates below, one-floor presentation, staging/spawn locations, plant-pocket size, prop placement, lighting allocation and tuning targets are **proposals to test**, not newly confirmed rules.

Any wall being eligible for reinforcement does not mean unlimited kits, free placement, instant construction or the ability to repair a missing wall. Use the accepted Session 08 rules when available. Existing prices/counts in Resources are tuning inputs, not map requirements. Do not silently balance this map by excluding some walls from eligibility.

This plan does not add a second map, another floor, jump/vault, rappelling, a roof route, arbitrary structural collapse, operators, moving doors, a new voice codec or a portal-acoustics engine. Floor/ceiling destructibility was not established by the user's wall requirement; keep that decision explicit and do not present it as settled. The prototype may use a continuous supporting floor and static roof slabs as a stated implementation assumption.

Use plan coordinates **(u, v)** in metres: u increases east/right, v increases south/down, north is the top of the drawing. Set the building's northwest corner to (0, 0). Its southeast corner is (32, 28).

For Godot, center the building horizontally:

```text
world X = u - 16
world Y = floor elevation + object height
world Z = v - 14
```

Thus the building spans X −16…16 and Z −14…14. The exterior ground envelope is u −4…36, v −4…38, or X −20…20 and Z −18…24. Ground is level at world Y = 0; spawn feet begin near Y = 0.05. Use **one conversion helper**, including for sites, spawns, props, cameras and test points. Never hand-convert the same coordinate independently in several scripts.

Reinforcement should initially retain the occupied wall volume so it cannot intersect a nearby player merely by thickening. ## 10. Universal destruction and reinforcement contract

### Coverage

Every wall surface in section 6 must resolve to a valid stable wall identity and gameplay damage/reinforcement target. Include exterior faces, courtyard faces, short closing pieces, headers, junctions and any later window frames that act as walls. Newly added decorative wall meshes must not become a second invisible or indestructible collider behind the damageable layer.

All authored Lightwell walls start as ordinary surfaces in the baseline layout. Reinforcement is a player action governed by Session 08; the purple samples in the comparison are not fixed reinforced locations. Reinforced walls remain destructible with the accepted counter-tool. Eligibility applies from both physically accessible faces unless an accepted rule explicitly says otherwise; validate range/LOS from the host's authoritative player position.

### Wall sizing and identity

Do not instantiate each 10–32 m run as one `WallModule` that only opens a single central doorway. Subdivide runs into bounded pieces, proposed maximum width approximately 2 m, with deliberate short end pieces. Use IDs such as `lightwell/v1/W07/seg_003` and distinct header/endcap IDs. Generate the same IDs/order and geometry on host/client; hash the complete map definition and include its identity in join compatibility checks.

Eligibility is universal even if effects/geometry are authored in bounded pieces. The exact damage granularity is a technical proposal, not permission to restrict destruction to a few marked panels. Stabilize segment IDs before collecting multiplayer evidence; a layout edit increments the map revision rather than reusing an ID for a different place.

### Complete wall removal gap in the current prototype

The inspected `WallModule` has INTACT→BREACHED, with a centered opening and surviving jamb/lintel parts; ordinary full-size panels cannot be damaged again once BREACHED. That does **not** by itself demonstrate eventual removal of an entire wall.

Before accepting this map, choose and implement one bounded scheme that lets all remaining wall material be removed. A straightforward candidate is an additional fully REMOVED authored state, with further qualified damage to the remaining parts. Another is individually damageable authored wall cells. Prefer the smaller complete change after testing one ordinary and one reinforced wall; do not introduce arbitrary runtime fracture.

Whichever scheme is used:

- Every surviving wall part is still damageable and has a route to zero wall collision/mesh. No permanent jamb, lintel, corner post or fake structural column may preserve a forbidden solid barrier.
- The exact tool/cost/number of actions to fully clear a wall is a tuning decision; do not invent a free second detonation or unlimited tools without documenting it.
- Reinforcement, partial damage and removal share one authoritative state/material/revision contract. Only valid transitions change inventory or spend allowance.
- Meshes, movement collision, bullet rays, voice occlusion and charge placement use the same current host state.
- Rejected, duplicated, concurrent, stale-round and out-of-range actions cannot consume tools twice, repair holes, close collision around a player or resurrect removed geometry.
- Reset restores the authored ordinary baseline and accepted economy state. Removed wall nodes/IDs remain addressable for snapshots/reset even if their collision/mesh is empty.

Audit decoration too: wall-bound trim, signs, cable runs, blinds and scorch/debris must disappear or update with the damaged part. Keep debris cosmetic and bounded. Floating invisible glass, metal mullions or baseboards cannot survive as an accidental wall.
